## App name

Physics Gacha

## Short description / subtitle

High-school physics drills — pick the law yourself

## Full description

Physics Gacha is a practice app that deals high-school physics problems one at a time.

Problems do not name the chapter. You read the situation and decide which law to use — mechanics, thermodynamics, waves, electromagnetism, or atomic physics. Hints, approach, first step, and a full solution are available when you need them.

Enter answers with a calculator (value and unit). Track history and optionally exclude problems you have already solved. Sign in to sync history and settings to the cloud. You can also ask the AI chat about the current problem.
